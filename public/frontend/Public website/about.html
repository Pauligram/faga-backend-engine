Our Story

Making everyday transactions simpler.

FAGA is built around a simple idea: people should not have to navigate a different platform for every everyday need.

Whether someone needs to send something, buy something, order a meal or find a trusted service provider, FAGA brings these experiences together through one platform.

Our ecosystem is designed to connect customers, businesses, riders, restaurants and service professionals while creating opportunities for people and businesses to participate in the digital economy.

What Drives Us

Our mission

FAGA is focused on making digital commerce and everyday services more accessible, connected and convenient.

Connect

Connect customers with products, restaurants, riders, businesses and service professionals through one platform.

Simplify

Reduce friction in everyday transactions by bringing discovery, ordering, payment and delivery closer together.

Create Opportunity

Give businesses, riders and service providers digital tools that can help them reach more customers and grow.
