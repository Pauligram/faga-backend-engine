Our approach to safety

Safety is a shared responsibility. FAGA works to provide tools, policies and processes that help customers, riders, sellers, restaurants and service providers interact responsibly on the platform.

Our safety features may include account verification, transaction monitoring, order tracking, reporting tools, platform policies, support channels and measures designed to detect or prevent abuse.

Safety basics for everyone

A few simple practices can help protect your account, your transactions and the people you interact with.

Protect your account

Keep your account credentials private and use strong, unique authentication information.

  • Never share your password or verification codes.
  • Review account activity regularly.
  • Report suspicious login activity.

Communicate carefully

Keep transaction-related communication within appropriate FAGA channels where available.

  • Be cautious with unexpected payment requests.
  • Do not share unnecessary personal information.
  • Report suspicious or abusive messages.

Verify locations

Confirm pickup and delivery details before a transaction begins.

  • Check addresses carefully.
  • Use available order tracking features.
  • Meet or hand over items in appropriate locations.

Watch for impersonation

FAGA personnel should not ask for sensitive credentials or unnecessary payment information.

  • Be cautious of unofficial support requests.
  • Do not disclose verification codes.
  • Use official support channels.

Review transactions

Check order details before confirming a purchase, booking or delivery.

  • Verify item or service details.
  • Review charges before payment.
  • Keep transaction records when appropriate.

Report concerns

Prompt reporting helps FAGA investigate potential safety, fraud or abuse issues.

  • Report suspicious activity promptly.
  • Provide relevant order information.
  • Contact emergency services for immediate emergencies.

Safety by role

Different FAGA participants have different responsibilities.

Customers

Help keep deliveries, purchases and service appointments safe.

  • Use accurate delivery and contact information.
  • Do not request prohibited goods or unlawful services.
  • Treat riders and providers respectfully.
  • Report unsafe behavior through official channels.

Riders & Drivers

Follow applicable road, vehicle, delivery and platform safety requirements.

  • Follow applicable traffic and transportation laws.
  • Never operate a vehicle while impaired.
  • Use appropriate safety equipment.
  • Do not accept prohibited or suspicious packages.

Sellers

Provide accurate product information and comply with applicable selling requirements.

  • List products accurately.
  • Do not sell prohibited or unlawful products.
  • Use appropriate packaging.
  • Handle customer information responsibly.

Restaurants

Follow applicable food safety and preparation requirements.

  • Follow applicable food safety regulations.
  • Package food appropriately for transport.
  • Provide accurate menu and allergen information where required.
  • Maintain appropriate food handling standards.

Service Providers

Provide services professionally and comply with applicable licensing requirements.

  • Maintain required qualifications and licenses.
  • Represent services accurately.
  • Respect customer privacy and property.
  • Follow applicable safety standards.

Everyone

Respect other people and help maintain a safe platform.

  • No harassment, threats or violence.
  • No fraud or deliberate platform abuse.
  • Respect personal boundaries.
  • Report serious safety concerns promptly.

Safer deliveries and pickups

Use these practical steps when sending or receiving items.

Sending an item

  • Provide accurate pickup and delivery information.
  • Package items securely and appropriately.
  • Accurately describe the package when requested.
  • Do not attempt to send prohibited or unlawful goods.
  • Keep relevant order information available until completion.

Receiving an item

  • Confirm the delivery details before accepting the item.
  • Use a safe and appropriate handover location.
  • Check the package for obvious damage where appropriate.
  • Report significant delivery problems promptly.
  • Do not make unnecessary payments outside approved channels.

Restricted and prohibited activity

FAGA may restrict or prohibit certain products, activities and transactions for legal, safety or operational reasons.

Illegal goods

Goods whose sale, possession or transportation is unlawful.

Dangerous materials

Hazardous materials that cannot safely be handled through the relevant service.

Fraudulent transactions

Transactions involving deception, stolen payment methods or deliberate platform abuse.

Unauthorized access

Attempts to access another person's account, data or systems.

Harassment

Threatening, abusive, discriminatory or intimidating behavior.

Misdeclared packages

Concealing or deliberately misrepresenting package contents.

Platform abuse

Malware, unauthorized automation, attacks or attempts to disrupt platform operations.

Off-platform scams

Attempts to redirect users into suspicious or unauthorized payment arrangements.
