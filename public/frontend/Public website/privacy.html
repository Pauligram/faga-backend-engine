The final production version should be reviewed and updated to reflect the exact FAGA legal entity, jurisdictions, products, data processors, retention schedules and regulatory requirements that apply to the deployed platform.

1. Introduction

Welcome to FAGA. FAGA provides a connected platform through which users may access or participate in logistics, marketplace, food and professional service experiences.

This Privacy Policy explains how information relating to identifiable individuals may be collected and processed when they interact with FAGA, including through our websites, applications, accounts, customer support channels and related services.

By using FAGA, you acknowledge that your information may be handled as described in this Privacy Policy, subject to applicable law and the specific terms presented to you when you use particular services.

2. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use FAGA and the features you choose to access.

Information you provide

  • Name and profile information.
  • Email address and phone number.
  • Account credentials and authentication information.
  • Delivery, billing and transaction details.
  • Addresses and delivery instructions.
  • Product, food, service or order information.
  • Information submitted when contacting customer support.
  • Business or professional information when registering as a seller, rider, service provider or restaurant.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser information.
  • IP address and approximate network information.
  • Platform usage and interaction information.
  • Log information, diagnostic information and security events.
  • Cookies and similar technologies where applicable.

Data minimization

FAGA should collect only information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant service, security, legal or operational purpose.

3. How We Use Information

Depending on the service and circumstances, FAGA may use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide, operate and improve FAGA services.
  • Process and manage orders, bookings and transactions.
  • Coordinate deliveries and logistics.
  • Connect customers with sellers, riders, restaurants and service providers.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, security threats and unauthorized activity.
  • Send transactional notifications and service communications.
  • Personalize or improve the user experience.
  • Perform analytics and understand platform performance.
  • Meet legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

4. Information Sharing

FAGA may share information when necessary to provide a requested service, operate the platform, protect users or comply with applicable law.

Service participants

Where necessary to fulfil an order or service, relevant information may be shared with the appropriate rider, seller, restaurant or service provider.

Service providers

FAGA may use third-party companies to support hosting, communications, payments, analytics, security, customer support and other operational functions.

Legal and safety purposes

Information may be disclosed where required or permitted by law, or where reasonably necessary to protect users, FAGA, the public or the integrity of the platform.

FAGA should not sell personal information merely because information is collected through the platform. Any legally relevant sharing or processing arrangement should be disclosed as required by applicable law.

5. Cookies & Similar Technologies

FAGA may use cookies, local storage, pixels, software development kits and similar technologies to support essential functionality, security, preferences, analytics and other permitted purposes.

Depending on the technology and applicable law, you may be able to control certain cookies or similar technologies through your browser or device settings.

Essential technologies

Some technologies may be required for functions such as authentication, security, shopping carts, session management and other core platform functionality.

6. Location Information

Certain FAGA services may require location information to support delivery, dispatch, mapping, service availability or other location dependent functionality.

Where applicable, location access may be controlled through your device or browser permissions.

FAGA should explain the purpose of location collection when permission is requested and should avoid collecting more precise location information than is reasonably necessary for the relevant service.

7. Payments

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers or financial service partners.

Payment providers may collect and process payment information under their own privacy policies and contractual arrangements.

Payment security

FAGA should not store full payment-card credentials in the browser or expose payment provider secrets through client-side code. Sensitive payment processing should be handled through appropriate secure payment systems.

8. Data Retention

FAGA may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain accounts, complete transactions, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, maintain security and comply with legal or regulatory requirements.

Specific retention periods should be determined according to the type of information, the purpose for which it was collected and applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, FAGA should securely delete it, anonymize it or otherwise dispose of it in accordance with its retention procedures.

9. Data Security

FAGA uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

Security measures may include access controls, encryption where appropriate, authentication controls, monitoring, logging, secure development practices and infrastructure protections.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their account credentials and promptly report suspected unauthorized activity.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have certain rights concerning your personal information.

Access

Request access to certain personal information held about you.

Correction

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.

Deletion

Request deletion where applicable and subject to legal exceptions.

Restriction

Request restriction of certain processing where permitted by law.

Portability

Request certain information in a portable format where legally applicable.

Marketing Choices

Control certain promotional communications where applicable.

Requests may be subject to identity verification, legal requirements and applicable exceptions.

11. Children's Privacy

FAGA's services are not intended for individuals who are not permitted to use the relevant service under applicable law.

FAGA should not knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is prohibited without the required consent or authorization.

If you believe that information relating to a child has been improperly submitted to FAGA, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

12. Third-Party Services

FAGA may integrate third-party services to provide functions such as payment processing, maps, communications, analytics, hosting, identity verification and customer support.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual obligations.

Users should review relevant third-party privacy notices where appropriate.

13. International Data Transfers

Depending on the services and infrastructure used by FAGA, information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where the user is located.

Where applicable, FAGA should implement appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers in accordance with relevant privacy and data protection requirements.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

FAGA may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the platform, business practices, technology, legal requirements or other relevant circumstances.

When material changes are made, FAGA may provide additional notice where required.

The "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this policy should be updated whenever the published policy is materially revised.

15. Contact Us

If you have a privacy question, request or concern, please contact FAGA through the official contact channels provided on our website.

For account, order or service-related issues, you may also use the FAGA Help Center or the support tools available within your account.

Have a privacy question?

Contact FAGA or visit the Help Center for assistance with privacy, account and platform questions.
